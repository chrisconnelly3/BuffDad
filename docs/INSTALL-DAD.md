# Installing BuffDad on Dad's iPhone

Do this together, in person or on the phone. Takes about a minute.

## Dad's app

1. Open **Safari** (has to be Safari, not Chrome) and go to the Dad URL.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. The name should already say **BuffDad** — tap **Add** in the top right.
5. Go to the home screen and open the app from the new **BuffDad** icon (not from Safari).
6. Tap **Turn on notifications** when it asks, then tap **Allow** on the iOS popup.

That's it — dad's set up.

## Chris's coach app

Same steps, with your own coach URL:

1. Open **Safari** and go to the Coach URL.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it from the **BuffDad Coach** icon on the home screen.
4. Tap **Turn on notifications** → **Allow**.

## The one gotcha

Notifications only work when the app is opened from the **home screen icon**. If you open the URL in Safari instead, "Turn on notifications" won't stick — always launch from the icon.
